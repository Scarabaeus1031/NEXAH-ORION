# NEXAH-ORION Licensing Scope

Licenses follow material type and provenance rather than applying one
undifferentiated license to every artifact.

| Material | Default license | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software, tests, configuration and implementation code | [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) | Original NEXAH-ORION software unless a file states otherwise |
| Original documentation, specifications, research, books and visual material | [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DOCS.md) | Original NEXAH-ORION material unless a file states otherwise |
| Third-party or source-derived material | Its stated source license | Not relicensed by NEXAH-ORION |
| Generated artifacts and reports | License of their authoritative source inputs | Generation does not create or transfer authority |

Repository location, rendering, indexing and generation do not change a
component's license. Local notices and provenance records take precedence.

Contributions follow the license of the destination material. Contributors
must identify externally sourced material and confirm that they may submit it
under the applicable terms.
