## Stability conclusion

The existing architecture remained stable across 12 independently identified,
versioned documents spanning 12 document types. No session required a new
Operator, public field, lifecycle outcome, authority rule or Gateway
responsibility.

The architecture is validated for deterministic, evidence-selected structural
orientation over repository-owned text documents.

## Limits of the claim

This evaluation does not prove:

- automatic evidence discovery;
- semantic document interpretation;
- free-language request translation;
- Human comprehension or usefulness through a rendered interface;
- externally governed scientific-paper or book ingestion;
- non-text Representation handling;
- provider independence across live reasoning systems.

These are coverage limits, not evidence of architectural failure. The frozen
public contract and architecture suites remain unchanged.
