## Transformation

- **Purpose:** make periodic modes, ratios, relative phase, and resonance relationships visible.
- **Conceptual operator:** discrete Fourier transform candidate documented in Phase 3B; no concrete signal selection or profile is authorized.
- **Required parameters:** selected signal/fields, sample coordinates/rate, interval, window, normalization.
- **Optional parameters:** detrending, zero padding, explicit spectral truncation.
- **Expected output:** deterministic frequency bins and coefficients bound to the selected signal and transform profile.
- **Known limitations:** the canonical Scarabaeus signal, Frequency Cross axes, sampling, and normalization are undocumented.

## Preserved Invariants

- **Identity:** selected source signal/path identity.
- **Provenance:** source samples, transform profile, preprocessing, and contract version.
- **Orientation Object ID:** unchanged.
- **Source references:** signal and sample references retained.
- **Epoch:** retained as metadata if supplied; not recoverable from a spectrum alone unless encoded.
- **Known constants:** sampling rate, interval, normalization, and window constants only when explicitly supplied.

## Lossiness

- **Information preserved:** frequency ratios, coefficients, and relative phase under a complete complex profile.
- **Information hidden:** time localization, spatial/state neighborhood, and Engine layout.
- **Information aggregated:** global sample interval into spectral coefficients.
- **Information discarded:** phase under magnitude-only output; bins under truncation; trends under declared detrending.
- **Unknown:** aliasing and omitted-source effects until sampling and signal profiles are evidenced.

## Evidence

- **Current evidence level:** `E1`; spectral analysis is a documented candidate, but concrete axes and inputs are absent.
- **Missing evidence:** canonical signal fields, sampling profile, Frequency Cross axes, normalization, phase/negative-frequency retention.
- **Required datasets:** versioned ordered signal fixtures with units, sample spacing, known declared components, phase, preprocessing profile, and expected spectrum metadata.
- **Open questions:** Which Engine signal is analyzed? Are complex phase and negative frequencies retained?

## Validation

- **Input validation:** ordered finite samples, signal identity/version, units, sample spacing, interval, transform parameters, provenance.
- **Output validation:** deterministic bins, units, coefficient representation, normalization/window/truncation declarations, source trace-back.
- **Invariant checks:** Orientation Object ID, frequency ratios, relative phase where retained, provenance, and reversible-profile declarations.
- **Failure conditions:** missing sampling metadata, hidden preprocessing, aliasing policy absent, incomplete parameters, undeclared phase loss, or execution while the candidate lacks approval.
