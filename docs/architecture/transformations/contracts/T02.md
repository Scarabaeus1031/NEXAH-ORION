## Transformation

- **Purpose:** expose explicit categorical correspondences for observed entities.
- **Conceptual operator:** relation `R ⊆ Observation × Category` from Phase 3B; its mapping rule is `unknown`.
- **Required parameters:** taxonomy ID/version, observation-to-category crosswalk, entity identity field, unit normalization, ambiguity/conflict policy.
- **Optional parameters:** relation weight, curator note, display order, aliases bound to canonical IDs.
- **Expected output:** provenance-linked category and relation entries; many-to-many mappings remain explicit.
- **Known limitations:** no authoritative taxonomy or crosswalk is available; category assignment cannot be executed.

## Preserved Invariants

- **Identity:** observed entity identity.
- **Provenance:** Observation, taxonomy, crosswalk, and mapping provenance.
- **Orientation Object ID:** unchanged.
- **Source references:** Observation and entity references retained.
- **Epoch:** preserved as metadata when available, otherwise `unknown`.
- **Known constants:** only taxonomy constants explicitly present in the future versioned crosswalk.

## Lossiness

- **Information preserved:** entity identity, selected correspondence, relation, ambiguity, and source trace-back.
- **Information hidden:** raw observation and instrument context when retained only by reference.
- **Information aggregated:** multiple observations or values mapped to one category.
- **Information discarded:** none is authorized; a target-only view may be unable to reconstruct the raw observation.
- **Unknown:** polarity, weighting, and mapping cardinality until the taxonomy and crosswalk exist.

## Evidence

- **Current evidence level:** `E0`; the representation is named but its crosswalk is absent.
- **Missing evidence:** authoritative taxonomy, field definitions, relation semantics, crosswalk, ambiguity rules.
- **Required datasets:** versioned observation-to-category mapping fixtures with entity IDs, units, source references, and counterexamples.
- **Open questions:** Are assignments one-to-one, many-to-many, weighted, or contextual? Which categories are data rather than metaphor?

## Validation

- **Input validation:** valid Observation identity/version, entity reference, field units, and provenance.
- **Output validation:** taxonomy and crosswalk versions, canonical category IDs, ambiguity status, and source trace-back.
- **Invariant checks:** Orientation Object ID equality, entity/source-reference preservation, provenance completeness, declared aggregation.
- **Failure conditions:** absent taxonomy/crosswalk, unmapped mandatory fields, silent conflict resolution, inferred categories, or execution while the operator is `unknown`.
