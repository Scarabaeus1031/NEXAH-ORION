# Vertical Slice IV — Responsibility Matrix

Status: Canonical architecture
Implementation status: Not started
Scope: Unique ownership at the Expression Boundary

## 1. Governing Rule

> Expression may communicate only what certified predecessors establish. It may
> neither repeat a predecessor's responsibility nor perform a successor's
> responsibility.

## 2. Responsibility Matrix

| Boundary or participant | One responsibility | May provide to Slice IV | Must never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certified ORION Core | Construct and certify deterministic structural Orientation | Passed Slice III certification and exact immutable certified artifacts | Change its outputs for communicability or acquire Human meaning |
| Vertical Slice III Certification | Establish the accepted entry gate and exact frozen lineage | `at_slice_iii_certified`, artifact identities, references, replay and provenance status | Express, interpret, render, or authorize downstream action |
| Expression | Faithfully make certified information communicable within declared scope | Traceable communication of certified fields, relations, boundaries, provenance, and absence | Infer semantics, mutate inputs, create Core structure, decide, or act |
| External Expression Conformance | Observe fidelity to exact certified inputs outside Expression construction | Accepted or rejected conformance result in a future implementation | Construct, repair, complete, normalize, or reinterpret expression |
| Slice IV Certification | In a future implementation, verify deterministic replay and frozen Expression responsibilities | Closing Slice IV status | Add expression content or downstream behavior |
| LYRA | Provide faithful Human-language expression of already-certified fields | Human-readable language preserving status, boundaries, and provenance | Orient, infer, decide, validate, route, contradict, or replace ORION |
| SIRIUS | Remain the later local access or action-context boundary | Nothing inside Slice IV | Become Expression, Core authority, or Human decision authority |
| Runtime | Remain outside this Slice IV architecture definition | Nothing required by this architecture | Be redesigned or extended by Slice IV |
| Applications | Present a future accepted expression | Presentation surface only | Alter certified content, conceal boundaries, or claim Core authority |
| NEXAHEDRON | Preserve the Human laboratory and presentation boundary | Human access to future accepted expression | Construct Core artifacts or reinterpret expression as new ORION output |
| Human | Own meaning, interpretation, judgment, acceptance, and action | Explicit acceptance or continuation outside Slice IV | Be silently replaced by software authority |

Each responsibility occurs once.

## 3. Input Responsibility

Expression may require only the minimum accepted set:

- passed Vertical Slice III Certification;
- accepted Orientation Map Conformance;
- exact Orientation Map Object;
- exact Constructed Orientation Map.

Upstream certified artifacts may be read only when their exact referenced fields
must be communicated. Expression may not retrieve or reconstruct a missing
artifact.

## 4. Output Responsibility

Expression may communicate:

- certified content;
- certified structure and order;
- certified Relations and adjacency;
- certified status and boundaries;
- certified provenance and references;
- declared absence, unknowns, exclusions, and lossiness.

Expression must declare the scope of what it communicates. It may not imply
completeness beyond that scope.

## 5. Authority Matrix

| Claim | Authority |
|---|---|
| Source identity and ownership | Human, source, and existing source authority |
| Structural Representation | Certified Representation Boundary |
| Inventory, Summary, and Statistics | Certified Slice II responsibilities |
| Relations | Certified Relations Layer |
| Navigation context | Certified Navigation Layer |
| Orientation Map structure | Certified Orientation Map Layer |
| Slice III reproducibility and lineage | Vertical Slice III Certification |
| Fidelity of communication | Slice IV Expression boundary |
| Human-language phrasing | LYRA within its frozen language boundary |
| Meaning and interpretation | Human |
| Acceptance and action | Human |

Expression owns no claim already owned by another row.

## 6. Non-Overlap Invariants

1. Expression does not become a second Orientation Map.
2. LYRA does not become the Expression authority; it remains the language
   specialization.
3. Presentation does not become Expression construction.
4. External Conformance does not become repair.
5. Certification does not become construction or validation.
6. SIRIUS does not move into Slice IV.
7. Runtime does not become an implicit dependency.
8. Human interpretation does not become software output.

## 7. Responsibility Acceptance

This matrix is complete when every future Slice IV decision can be assigned to
exactly one existing row without modifying the certified Core or granting
Expression semantic authority.
